Five document tools, no server in between.

Nyxdesk opens, edits, and saves the document formats people already send you — text, spreadsheets, slides, notes, PDFs — without an account, a subscription, or a server that ever sees your files.

What's actually here

This isn't a reimplementation of a large commercial office suite — that's a multi-decade undertaking, and claiming otherwise wouldn't be honest. It's a working set of five tools covering the parts of that job people reach for most often, built on well-established open formats so what you save opens elsewhere too.

Everything autosaves to your browser's local storage as you work. Closing the tab won't lose your place; a different browser or device will start fresh, since nothing syncs anywhere.

What's on the roadmap

  • Cross-sheet formula references and basic charts in Sheets
  • Native tables in Docs, beyond the text-grid fallback
  • Layout- and image-preserving PDF and slide import
  • Page reordering and multi-file merge in the PDF tool
  • Optional peer-to-peer sync for people who want it — still no required server

Nyxdesk reads and writes files compatible with widely used office document formats, for interoperability only. It is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any software vendor, and uses no vendor names, logos, or branding.
